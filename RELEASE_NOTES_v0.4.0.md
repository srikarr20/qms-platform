
## External Software and Experimental Data

### Pyxel Detector Simulation Framework

QMS includes a separate integration with the Pyxel detector-simulation framework.

Pyxel is an open-source detector simulation framework developed at the European Space Agency (ESA), in collaboration with the European Southern Observatory (ESO), for modeling and combining detector effects in imaging systems.

Official resources:

- Project website: https://esa.gitlab.io/pyxel/
- Source repository: https://gitlab.com/esa/pyxel
- Documentation: https://esa.gitlab.io/pyxel/doc/latest/

Primary publication:

Arko, M., Prod'homme, T., Lemmel, F., Serra, B., George, E. M., Kelman, B.,
Pichon, T., Biancalani, E., & Gilbert, J. (2022).

Pyxel 1.0: an open source Python framework for detector and end-to-end
instrument simulation.

Journal of Astronomical Telescopes, Instruments, and Systems,
8(4), 048002.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.4.048002

QMS uses Pyxel as an external detector forward-model environment.
Pyxel is not part of QMS, and the QMS project is not presented as an
ESA-developed or ESA-validated system.

### University of Glasgow Experimental Measurements

QMS includes a separate adapter and causal-replay analysis pathway for archival
single-photon experimental measurements from the University of Glasgow.

The current regression uses:

`Heralded Diffraction SM.zip`

Verified dataset citation:

Aspden, R. S., Padgett, M., & Spalding, G. (2016).

*Video recording true single-photon double-slit interference* [Data collection].

University of Glasgow.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5525/gla.researchdata.281

Associated publication:

Aspden, R. S., Padgett, M. J., & Spalding, G. C. (2016).

*Video recording true single-photon double-slit interference.*

American Journal of Physics, 84(9), 671–677.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4955173

Official resources:

- University of Glasgow: https://www.gla.ac.uk/
- School of Physics & Astronomy: https://www.gla.ac.uk/schools/physics/
- Enlighten Research Data: https://researchdata.gla.ac.uk/

The measurements were produced independently of QMS and are used for
retrospective causal-replay analysis. QMS does not claim authorship of the data
or affiliation with the University of Glasgow.
